Help us find the problemInclude what happened, your app version and any error or request ID. For token or privacy questions, use Settings > Contact support in the app to include your support identifier. Never send passwords, payment-card details or private photos.

Current release: 0.8.7 · 4 November 2026This release has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Pro paywalls, token purchases, new AI generation and advertising tracking are disabled. Existing saved artwork remains editable. References below to purchases, tokens, AI uploads or ad measurement describe features from earlier versions and do not apply to this release.

Earlier purchases

  • Cancel a subscription: iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions > CarCustoms. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
  • Refunds: App Store purchases are refunded by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Purchases and restore controls are unavailable in this release. Email support for questions about earlier purchases or tokens.
  • Tokens: a token is charged after a Me & My Car image is created successfully. If the download is interrupted, retry the same request. If a charge or refund is unclear, email us with the time and request ID.

Privacy requests

CarCustoms has no accounts, so most of your data is on your iPhone and is removed when you delete the app. To delete token or image data on our server, use Settings > Contact support in the app. The email includes your support identifier so we can locate your records. If you no longer have the app, email us directly and explain your request.
