Where can I use my widgets?

Use widgets on your iPhone Home Screen and in StandBy. CarPlay widgets require iOS 26 or later and a compatible vehicle. They appear in Apple’s widget area.

Is this the CarPlay dashboard?

The preview shows CarCustoms’ Drive view on iPhone. It does not replace the full Apple CarPlay dashboard. The layout and supported interactions differ on the car screen.

Can I use my own car?

Yes. Add a rear-view photo in the app and personalize its label. The website preview uses a concept car illustration.

Are there any purchases?

No. This release has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. New AI image generation is temporarily unavailable; you can still customize widgets with your own photos and edit saved artwork.
