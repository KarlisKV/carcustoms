1. Who we are

CarCustoms is published by RVS Commerce, SIA, a company registered in Latvia (registration number 40203766164), Brīvības iela 40-27, Rīga, LV-1050, Latvia ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: admin@ecomagain.com.

2. The short version

  • CarCustoms works without an account. We never ask for your name or email in the app.
  • Your widget settings, background photos, recordings, calendar and birth details stay on your iPhone. Connected map and weather features send location information to their providers, as explained below.
  • If you use Me & My Car, the two photos you pick are sent to Google Gemini through our server to create your image. Our generation service does not store those uploaded photos; Google processes them under its API terms.
  • Purchases run through Apple. Our subscription and token providers (Superwall and RevenueCat) know your purchases and a random app identifier.
  • Ad measurement is off unless you turn it on and allow tracking in the iOS prompt.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

3. Data that stays on your iPhone

We do not receive the local data listed below. Some features also contact external providers; section 4 explains what those providers receive.

  • Location. Used for your position on the map, directions, trip distance, local weather and Game Radar. Game Radar records a trail only after you tap Start recording and keeps running in the background until you stop it. The recent trail stays in memory; the last map image is saved for its widget until you tap Clear trail.
  • Microphone. Used only when you tap Start recording to save a drive sound of up to 10 seconds. The recording stays on your device.
  • Calendar. Read to show events in the calendar widgets you choose. You can disconnect the calendar in the app.
  • Motion. Used to show acceleration in your dashboard. Not stored.
  • Apple Music. Used to show and control playback.
  • Photos for widget backgrounds. Picked with Apple's photo picker (we do not get access to your whole library) and stored in the app's container.
  • Birth date (horoscope widget). Birth date, time and place are used on your device to personalize the horoscope widget.

5. Me & My Car (AI images)

When you create a Me & My Car image, the app shows what will happen and waits for you to confirm. Then:

  • The person photo and car photo you selected are resized, rotated to the right orientation, and stripped of location metadata on your iPhone.
  • They are sent over an encrypted connection to our generation service (run on Cloudflare) and on to Google's Gemini API, which creates the image.
  • Our service does not store the photos you upload. The generated image is kept on our server for 7 days so an interrupted download can be recovered, then deleted. A copy is saved on your iPhone for reuse.
  • Google processes the photos and generated images under the Gemini API terms. Google may retain them for a limited period for safety, abuse prevention and legal obligations. Our seven-day retention period applies to our generation service, not to Google’s separate processing.
  • The image is created by AI and may not look exactly like you or your car.

Please only upload photos of yourself, or of people who agreed, and of cars you have the right to photograph.

6. Purchases and tokens

Subscriptions (weekly and yearly) and token packs are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment; we never see your card details. Superwall shows the paywall and checks whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat keeps your token balance. Both work with a random identifier created on your iPhone, not your name or Apple ID.

7. Ad measurement and tracking

"Allow ad measurement" in the app is off by default. If you turn it on, iOS asks whether you allow tracking. Only if you allow both do we share your device identifier and paywall and purchase events with Superwall and Meta to learn which ads lead to installs and subscriptions. You can turn it off any time in the app or in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Turning it off does not affect your purchases or widgets. Without permission, ad attribution is limited to Apple's privacy-preserving systems (SKAdNetwork).

9. Why we use your data (legal bases)

If you are in the EU or UK, we rely on:

  • Contract: to provide the app, your purchases, tokens and the images you request.
  • Consent: for location, microphone, calendar and other permissions, for sending photos to the AI service, and for ad measurement. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing done before.
  • Legitimate interest: to keep our service secure and working (operational logs), to answer support requests.
  • Legal obligation: to keep purchase records as tax law requires.

10. How long we keep data

  • Uploaded photos: not stored by our service.
  • Generated images on our server: 7 days. Request-status records may remain after image deletion to prevent duplicate token charges and resolve support requests.
  • Token balance and transaction history: as long as you have tokens or until you ask us to delete them, plus what tax law requires.
  • Operational logs in Cloudflare Workers Logs: up to 7 days under its current retention limits. These logs describe request outcomes and errors; our application does not write uploaded photos to them.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after the last message.
  • Data on your iPhone: until you delete it or the app.

11. International transfers

Some of our providers are in the United States. Where data leaves the European Economic Area we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

12. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Because the app has no accounts, most of your data is on your iPhone and you control it directly. To delete server-side data (token account, generated images), use Settings > Contact support in the app. The email includes your support identifier so we can locate the relevant records. You can also email admin@ecomagain.com directly; we may need more information to find your records. We reply within one month.

You can complain to the Data State Inspectorate of Latvia (Datu valsts inspekcija, dvi.gov.lv) or the authority in your country.

US residents (including California). We do not sell personal information. If you allow ad measurement, sharing your device identifier with Meta can count as "sharing" for cross-context advertising under some state laws; you can opt out at any time with the in-app toggle or iOS tracking setting.

13. Children

CarCustoms is not directed to children. You must be at least 13 to use it. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us data (for example photos through Me & My Car), contact us and we will delete it.

14. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Your app identifier is stored in the iPhone Keychain. Photos are stripped of location metadata before upload. Access to our service is limited to what it needs to run.

15. Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
